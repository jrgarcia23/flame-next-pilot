What is people counting software?

People counting software measures how many people enter, leave and move through a physical space, and turns those counts into business metrics such as conversion rate, dwell time and occupancy. The footfall counter is the device that detects people; the software is the layer that cleans, stores and analyses the data across all your sites.

In 2026, a professional platform should cover at least six functions:

  • Entry and exit counting with staff exclusion and correct handling of groups.
  • Real-time and historical dashboards by entrance, floor and site.
  • Conversion rate, by connecting footfall with POS or ERP sales.
  • Zone analytics: flow between areas, dwell time and heatmaps.
  • Privacy by design under GDPR and the EU AI Act, without biometric identification.
  • APIs or exports to BI, CRM and marketing tools.

How to choose people counting software in 2026

Choose people counting software by testing accuracy in your own store first, then checking hardware, privacy, integrations, multi-site management and total cost. These six criteria cover most procurement processes:

  1. Accuracy verified in your store. Vendor figures (98%, 99%, 95-99%) are measured in their own conditions. Ask for a pilot with a manual count audit at your busiest entrance and hour.
  2. Existing cameras or new sensors. List your cameras first: resolution, height and angle. If they qualify, AI on existing cameras removes the hardware line; if not, compare 3D sensors.
  3. GDPR and the EU AI Act without biometrics. Counting does not need facial recognition. Ask each vendor whether it creates any individual template or re-identification signature, and where data is processed. Our GDPR video analytics compliance guide lists the questions to ask.
  4. Integrations with POS and BI. Conversion needs sales data. Check native connectors, REST API and scheduled exports.
  5. Multi-site and multi-country management. One dashboard, permissions by region or single sign-on (SSO), and KPIs that are comparable across stores and countries.
  6. Total cost of ownership. Add hardware, installation, licences, maintenance and replacement over three to five years, not only the first-year licence.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most accurate people counting technology?

There is no single winner: accuracy depends on the installation as much as on the technology. Vendors publish their own figures: RetailNext 95-99% for its Aurora sensor, V-Count up to 99% for Nano AI, Sensormatic up to 98% for ShopperTrak, and Flame Analytics over 99% for AI on existing cameras. Flame cites 85-90% for infrared and 80-90% for thermal sensors. Validate any figure with a manual count in your own store.

Can I use my existing CCTV cameras for people counting?

Yes, with software that applies AI video analytics to existing IP cameras. Flame Analytics connects via RTSP to cameras of at least 720p mounted top-down or angled from above, and runs a free audit to confirm compatibility. Sensor-based vendors such as V-Count and RetailNext rely on their own devices, while FootfallCam reuses existing cameras for some supermarket use cases.

Is people counting GDPR compliant without biometrics?

It can be. The EDPB video guidelines (3/2019) state that processing which distinguishes categories of people without uniquely identifying anyone does not fall under Article 9 GDPR. Images are still processed, so you need a legal basis, clear signage and usually a data protection impact assessment. Choose software that counts anonymous silhouettes and stores no images or biometric templates.

How much does a people counting system cost?

Cost depends on the number of entrances, the hardware and the analytics modules. According to Flame Analytics, dedicated sensors (infrared, thermal or stereoscopic) cost 500 to 2,000 euros per counting point before software licences. Reusing existing cameras removes that hardware line when they meet the requirements. Ask every vendor for a quote that covers hardware, installation, licences and maintenance.

What is the difference between a footfall counter and people counting software?

A footfall counter is the device that detects people crossing a line, such as a beam, thermal or 3D sensor. People counting software collects counts from devices or cameras across sites and turns them into metrics: hourly footfall, conversion with POS data, dwell time, occupancy and store benchmarks. Some vendors sell both; AI video platforms use your cameras as the counter.

Which people counting software works across multiple stores and countries?

All five platforms in this comparison manage multiple sites from the cloud. RetailNext reports 600+ retailers in 100+ countries, V-Count 600+ clients in 130+ countries, and Flame Analytics 90+ B2B customers in 12 countries with more than 500 stores measured. For European networks, check where data is processed, single sign-on and KPIs that are comparable across countries.

How long does it take to deploy people counting software?

It depends on the hardware. With existing cameras, Flame Analytics delivers live data in 5-7 business days per site: camera audit and RTSP connection, model calibration, then dashboards and training. Sensor-based rollouts need physical installation at every entrance, and V-Count notes that there is no universal installation time. Multi-site projects are usually rolled out in phases.
