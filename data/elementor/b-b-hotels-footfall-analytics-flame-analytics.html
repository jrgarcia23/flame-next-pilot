The case

B&B Hotels, one of the fastest-growing international hotel chains in Europe, wanted to evolve the digital experience offered to its guests and improve the relationship with its customers beyond the moment of booking or the stay.

In a context where travelers expect simple, fast and personalized digital experiences, the chain needed a solution that would provide a quality WiFi connection and, at the same time, generate useful knowledge about its guests.

To achieve this, B&B Hotels relied on Flame Analytics and its WiFi Marketing solution, adding a personalized connection experience capable of turning WiFi access into a digital touchpoint with the customer.

01 The challenge

Moving from a functional WiFi connection to a digital relationship with the guest

WiFi connectivity has become one of the most valued services among hotel customers.

However, for a chain with multiple properties, the real value lies in leveraging that interaction to better understand the guest and build longer-lasting relationships.

B&B Hotels wanted to answer key questions:

  • How to offer a simple, quality WiFi connection?
  • How to better understand the profile of its guests?
  • How to build its own database with consented information?
  • How to segment different customer profiles?
  • How to improve communication before, during and after the stay?
  • How to foster a more direct relationship with the guest?
The goal was to turn a basic service like WiFi into a strategic tool to improve experience and loyalty.
02 The solution

A smart Guest WiFi connected to the customer strategy

Flame Analytics deployed a WiFi Marketing solution that lets B&B Hotels offer a personalized connection experience and use that interaction to enrich guest knowledge.

Through a brand-adapted WiFi portal, users can connect easily while the chain gathers information provided with consent to improve the relationship with its customers.

The platform makes it possible to:

  • Capture the guests' own data.
  • Create audience profiles and segments.
  • Analyze customer information.
  • Integrate the data with the CRM ecosystem.
  • Activate more relevant communications.

This layer of knowledge helps B&B Hotels move toward a more personalized experience focused on the needs of each guest.

From guest data to a personalized experience

Thanks to the Flame Analytics platform, B&B Hotels can create segmented audiences and develop communication actions tailored to different moments of the customer relationship.

The information gathered makes it possible to work on different scenarios:

  • Communication before the stay.
  • The relationship during the visit.
  • Actions after the stay.
  • Loyalty of recurring customers.
  • Improving knowledge of the guest profile.
The integration of connectivity, data and marketing makes it possible to turn every digital interaction into an opportunity to improve the experience.
03 In progress

A more connected and personalized hotel experience

With Flame Analytics, B&B Hotels has a new way to connect with its guests and better understand their needs.

The solution makes it possible to move from a relationship based solely on the stay to a more connected model, where data helps improve communication and create more relevant experiences.

Teams can use this information to:

  • Improve the guest's digital experience.
  • Better understand customer profiles.
  • Design more suitable communications.
  • Increase loyalty.
  • Strengthen the direct relationship with users.

From the WiFi connection to guest intelligence

The project marks a step forward in the digitalization of B&B Hotels' hotel experience.

The WiFi connection stops being merely a necessary infrastructure and becomes a strategic channel to get to know the customer, generate engagement and build stronger relationships.

Thanks to Flame Analytics, B&B Hotels turns every connection into an opportunity to improve the guest experience and move toward a more personalized, data-driven hospitality.
